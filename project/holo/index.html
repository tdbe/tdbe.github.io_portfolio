Solo Design Project
June 2013

|A Concept Art & Design project, a game slice communicating how both the environment and gameplay come across.


| Pitch: jello version of xmen armor force-field suits, both attack and defense resource. Class and suit visuals are a consequence of the character's persona.

| The core mechanics: Suit morphs for attacks but its volume is limited. Player decides between defence and offence.

Unmetered freedom to spam your most powerful attacks.
But exposed body is punishable / vulnerable, suit can deplete / shrink / be sliced.

Game concept: 2.5D beat-em-up platformer. With drop-in co-op and RPG elements of Monster Hunter + capcom D&D or Dragon's Crown. The openness and depth depend on the tech and procedural content vs keyframing & writing.
